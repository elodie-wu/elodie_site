# Original Low-Poly Cat Mascot Candidates — V1

Generation mode: OpenAI ImageGen, stylized concept art.

## Shared direction

Create an original small low-poly 3D cat mascot for a personal software developer portfolio. Use a cute early-2000s game aesthetic: an oversized softly faceted cat head, tiny compact animal body, triangular ears, short paws and tail, visible polygon planes, low-resolution hand-painted textures, and soft studio lighting on a pale lavender-gray background. The character must remain animal-like, with no human hair or human clothes.

Compose each candidate as a square character concept card with one large three-quarter full-body pose and three smaller, consistent expression studies along the bottom: neutral, sleepy, and delighted. Do not include text, branding, watermarks, rooms, or additional characters.
